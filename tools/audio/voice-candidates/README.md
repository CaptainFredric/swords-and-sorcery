# Spellblade voice-line candidates — recording backlog

These are **unrecorded, provisional lines written by the creator**. Review the wording and delivery before declaring them in `client/game/sound/voiceLines.mjs` or recording them. This page is an idea shortlist, **not** an implemented trigger list or an authorization to add new gameplay mechanics.

## So-Fist-ication — prospective convoy / nearby allies

**Scenario:** Other allied Spellblades are near the player-operated ram. The operator feels compelled to outpace or outshine them. These lines are candidates for a future escort/convoy context; no proximity trigger or gameplay buff is specified or implemented here.

### Shortlist

- **Vultures!** “Vultures! You attempt to match something that you should not!”
  - **Intended moment:** Allies approach or keep pace with the ram.
  - **Performance consideration:** An indignant accusation, not a tactical warning. Preserve “something”; its impersonal self-aggrandizement is intentional.
  - **Status:** Strong candidate; latest user-selected wording.

- **Impossible Failure.** “You remind me of something which cannot happen to me: failure.”
  - **Intended moment:** Allies close in, provoking an involuntary concern about comparison and losing.
  - **Performance consideration:** The joke is his denial of failure while admitting it has occurred to him. Use sparingly, if retained.
  - **Status:** Alternate; lightly normalized punctuation.

## So-Fist-ication — prospective convoy / no allies nearby

**Scenario:** The ram operator finds himself unobserved, and considers effort less worthwhile without an audience.

### Shortlist

- **Unacknowledged Effort.** “I should hardly move when I will not be acknowledged for it.”
  - **Intended moment:** Few or no allies close enough to witness the operator.
  - **Performance consideration:** Matter-of-fact self-importance. A potential quieter/default line.
  - **Status:** Strong candidate; preserved wording.

- **Bad for Whom?** “I'm glad no one is around me. That would look bad. For them. No—for MYSELF!”
  - **Intended moment:** A rare, rambling self-correction while alone.
  - **Performance consideration:** Allow the sudden reversal to carry the joke; don't flatten into one continuous boast.
  - **Status:** Alternate; punctuation adjusted.

- **Persuasive Machine.** “Ah, no one to gawk at my persuasive machine? Then I shall take my time.”
  - **Intended moment:** Alone in the operator position.
  - **Performance consideration:** The ram is proudly described as persuasive; “my time” is a grammatical/meaning correction to the draft's “your time.”
  - **Status:** Optional; review this wording before recording.

## Deferred decisions

- **Do not automatically implement proximity-based driving bonuses.** A possible future convoy mechanic is that nearby allies encourage the operator to sustain Drive Pressure or operate more aggressively, but no final values or behavior have been approved.
- **Do not turn these into automatic, frequent barks.** Check repetition, overlap with urgent combat lines, and whether the player is actually operating So-Fist-ication.
- **Record only after a later creator review.** Once selected, follow [the recording instructions](../RECORDING.md) for declaration, takes, processing, subtitles, and verification.
- The more literal line about “potential failures” and the “last placer” line have been left out of this shortlist; their intended characterization/wording was weaker or ambiguous. They are not deleted from the original discussion.

NO MANUAL REQUIRED

READ IT. FIGHT.

01

SWORD

Hold attack for a three-hit chain. Walls stop the blade. Misses still commit the swing.

02

GUARD / PARRY

Guard drains stamina. Raise it at the last moment for the sharper, louder perfect parry.

03

FIREBALL

A visible projectile, not hitscan. Dodge it, use cover, or force movement with splash pressure.

04

DASH

A short directional burst with no invulnerability. Use it for spacing, pursuit and the bridge gap.

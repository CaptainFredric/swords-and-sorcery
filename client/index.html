Four clean sword hits defeat a full-health Spellblade (or three and a spell). Fireball keeps burning after it lands; Frostfire slows whoever it strikes until they thaw. A well-timed Guard parries and staggers the attacker. Spells beat passive blocking. Dash repositions; it does not grant invulnerability. Guard and Sprint share one stamina bar: sprinting drains it slowly, every block costs a chunk, and it refills a moment after you stop.

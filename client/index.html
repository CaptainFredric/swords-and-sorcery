Three sword hits defeat a full-health Spellblade. A well-timed Guard parries and staggers the attacker. Fireball beats passive blocking. Dash repositions; it does not grant invulnerability. Guard and Sprint share one stamina bar: sprinting drains it slowly, every block costs a chunk, and it refills a moment after you stop.

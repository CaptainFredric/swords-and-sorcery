# BiBye terrain implementation plan

Extend existing draft PR219 from e901924. Preserve the verified v4.1 binary and rollback files, authoritative clocks, damage pools, Ackermann steering and ordinary combat. Implement directly in the existing isolated worktree. No review agents, merge, deployment or publication.

The selected approach is a deterministic kinematic wheel contact solver integrated with the current host movement and collision. Increasing the old height cutoff cannot represent wheel contact or chassis attitude. A general rigid body engine would replace too much established authority and introduce avoidable integration risk.

1. Add regression scenarios through the real movement entry point for smooth grades, cross slopes, reversing, transitions, .15/.25/.35m obstacles, walls, gaps, damaged wheels and deployment. First reproduce missing attitude and failed curb traversal. Retain the existing flat combat baseline.
2. Add shared terrain support around the six actual wheel pivots. Use the .55m tire circle and actual track/wheelbase, continuous contact over surface edges, bounded articulation and a fitted chassis pose. Subdivide travel/turning to prevent tunneling, preserve occupied collision pieces and reject excessive grades, risers, belly strikes and unsupported drops. Apply an uphill speed penalty in addition to existing damage penalties.
3. Carry chassis pitch/roll and individual contact offsets through shared frames, snapshots and interpolation. Invert desired world aim into the inclined joints, preserve physical joint ranges, projectile deadlines and convergence. Keep the operator and mounted views in the same inclined frame. Inspect actual v4.1 motion before finalizing articulation limits.
4. Validate functioning stabilizer pads at their actual contact positions before deployment. Begin with an8degree grade ceiling and modest reachable hinge adjustment verified against the mesh. Reject unsupported or uneven planting with a short HUD reason; retain damaged support restrictions.
5. Play the actual authored GLB on an explicit terrain fixture using ordinary browser controls. Capture uphill/downhill/reverse/cross slope/curb/deploy/fire/damage behavior and operator fit. Compare frame pacing with e901924 under matching settings. Run relevant regressions, npm run verify and HTML5 export, then push the same draft with evidence and honest limits.

Acceptance includes finite continuous poses, supported wheels, clear body/obstacle separation, consistent inclined world muzzles and operator attachment, ordinary independent rear fire, safe deployment rejection and preserved mechanical clocks. The22degree/.25m targets remain subject to the real carriage geometry. Physical phone and live release validation are separate from this private branch.

The creator additionally requests immediate track appearance after upper recock, smaller practical input dead zones, smooth vehicle response and reticle calibration. Deployed Main mode will align both weapons during aiming rather than beginning a separate alignment after Fire. Preserve independent upper access during reload and existing two stage release deadlines. Test physical muzzle versus reticle rays at multiple ranges, including terrain inclination, and inspect both keyboard/mouse and touch.

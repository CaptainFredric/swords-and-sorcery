# Renowner and arena integration

The canonical runtime file comes directly from Argon's Rightful Renowner GameReady and Source Drive Backup v2 archive. The 6,281,348 byte GLB has SHA256 `2a9e73a7b5991757f851dd7e9148e7f91f5abc07283ae9792b50f9144b1983a2`. It contains 19 nodes, 18 meshes, 52,755 triangles, a PBR material with four embedded textures, six animation clips and a native 4.5 m height. The archive includes the preview, integration contract and Python reconstruction scripts. It lacks `base_basic_pbr.glb`, `base_basic_shaded.glb`, `texture_emissive.png` and `Rightful_Renowner_Flat_Entry_Primary.png` referenced by those scripts and has no Blender source or portable rebuild. It also lacks `07_Judgment_Sword_Execution` and `08_Judgment_Evaded_Coin_Retaliation`.

Court plays the original ceremony, reflecting alternate cycles with the greatsword parented to the corresponding wrist. The judgment poses extend the actual authored rig in response to server cues. The model owns no verdict, damage or reward logic. The flat runner is omitted on both installations because the existing podium geometry uses different levels.

Restless Day uses the same GLB fitted to the monumental base. During contact the blade follows the shared server segment, including the moving sweep angle. The reach endpoints, attack schedule, damage and health are preserved. The segment now starts at the attainable two handed grip and the real coin originates from the dispenser. This changes the near body portion of the blade and the coin launch position intentionally. The renderer stretches the blade below its guard and leaves the handle at the authored size.

## Arena eligibility

Castleward and the Ruined Keep remain in their original modes. The Court of Merit adds a compact arena with a central approach and flank lanes around the columns. Blackbell Foundry uses the Chain Yard's neutral spawns with the center lane, elevated gantry and lower trench. These two maps enter Bot Duel, matchmade Duel rotation, private and public Free for All, and Practice. Their normal matches have no active trial or capture objectives.

High Grounds is available in Practice, Shared Practice and Inspection with local starts at the Crown Span. Repositioning targets the Crown Span, Western Canopy, Lower Root Shelf and Azure Siege Road through supported, unoccupied positions. Dummies can be summoned again near the selected area. Wheels and deployment still use ordinary clearance checks, so use the Crown Span or Siege Road for builds. High Grounds stays in its existing competitive team mode and remains outside ordinary Duel rotation. Outerworks stays a Breakthrough map because its long staged approach and asymmetric team positions lack a vetted neutral subarea. Restless Day and Edge of Victory retain their own world rules.

## Oubloodlet integration gate

Oubloodlet remains a proposed world. To register it, supply the art and a shared authoritative collision world with explicit floor heights, ramps or stairs, bridge solids and an abyss plane beneath the lowest recoverable ledges. Author Upper Ward galleries, Middle Ward crossings and Lower Ward passages with two traversable routes between each significant level. Provide neutral spawn sets, safe respawn sites, and navigation links validated in both directions at runtime movement limits. Test displacement onto recoverable lower shelves separately from lethal descent into the bottom pit. Any moving hoist or breakable crossing needs server owned collision and updated navigation before it can be part of a required route. Evaluate Duel and Bot Duel first. Free for All requires a wider spawn and recovery audit. This branch registers no Oubloodlet map.

Sword in one hand. Sorcery in the other.

Swords & Sorcery is a free browser arena game set in Castleward. Close the distance with your blade, catch a strike on Guard, then answer with a spell. The Spellblade has opinions about honor. His opponents have objections.

Enter the yard

  • Duel a friend: create a private match and share its room code. No accounts or installs are needed.
  • Find a fight: seek a duel or browse open rooms.
  • Play solo: fight a bot at several difficulty levels, or use the Practice Yard to try moves against dummies.
  • Choose your kit: equip a spell and ultimate in the Armory, and customize your knight.

Learn a few moves

WASD moves. Shift sprints. Space jumps. C crouches.
Left mouse attacks. Right mouse or G raises Guard.
Q casts your equipped spell. E Dashes. R uses your charged ultimate.
Escape opens the match menu. Controls can be rebound in Settings.

During Spells & Chivalry, hold Q and press 1, 2 or 3 to choose a prepared spell. Tap Q to cast it. Touch controls are available for mobile play; landscape gives the arena more room.

A playable pre alpha

Combat, animation, presentation and browser compatibility are still being refined. Multiplayer uses the same live server as the standalone game. It may take about a minute to wake. Solo practice and bot play remain available while it connects.

If something breaks, please describe the device and browser, the move you attempted, and what happened. A short clip or screenshot helps.

Creator

Created, designed and performed by CaptainFredric. The character concepts, mechanics, dialogue and Spellblade voice are his original contributions. Generative AI tools assist implementation, artwork and interface copy through extensive direction, iteration and review. The recordings use the creator’s own voice; the game’s procedural audio is produced by its sound engine.

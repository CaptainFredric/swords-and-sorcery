Sword in one hand. Sorcery in the other.

Swords & Sorcery is a free first person multiplayer arena game set in Castleward. Duel with swords, block and parry attacks, and answer with Fireball, Frostfire, or another spell. The Spellblade has strong opinions about honor. His opponents have objections.

Choose your first fight

  • Duel a friend: Create a Private Match and share the room code. No account or installation is required.
  • Find opponents: Seek a Duel or browse Open Rooms.
  • Learn or practice: Choose Solo for Greener Pastures, bot duels, or the Practice Yard. Greener Pastures is an optional, fully voiced tutorial. Solo combat runs locally in your browser.

Playing in the browser

On desktop, fullscreen is recommended. Click the arena to control the camera. Default controls: WASD to move, left mouse button to attack, right mouse button or G to Guard, Q to cast, and E to Dash. Escape opens options. See How to Play for the remaining controls and Settings to change your key bindings.

Touch controls are available; landscape is recommended. Browser support is still being refined. Multiplayer may take a moment to connect after the server has been inactive. Solo remains available once the game loads.

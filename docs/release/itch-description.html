Sword in one hand. Sorcery in the other.

Swords & Sorcery is a free first person multiplayer arena game set in Castleward. Duel with swords, catch a strike on Guard, and answer with Fireball, Frostfire or another spell. The Spellblade has strong opinions about honor. His opponents have objections.

Play the standalone browser version

Choose your first fight

  • Duel a friend: create a Private Match and share its room code. No account or installation is required.
  • Find opponents: Seek a Duel or browse Open Rooms.
  • Learn or practice: choose Solo for the optional voiced Greener Pastures tutorial, bot duels or the Practice Yard. Solo combat runs locally in your browser.

Playing in the browser

On desktop, use fullscreen and click the arena to control the camera. Default controls: WASD to move, left mouse to attack, right mouse or G to Guard, Q to cast and E to Dash. Escape opens options. How to Play and Settings cover the remaining controls and rebinding.

Touch controls are available; landscape is recommended. Browser compatibility is still being refined. The multiplayer server may need time to wake after inactivity; solo modes remain available once the game loads.

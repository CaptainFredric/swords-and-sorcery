Welcome to Castleward!

Making Swords & Sorcery, one duel and questionable knightly declaration at a time.

I’m CaptainFredric, creator of Swords & Sorcery, and the designer and voice behind the Spellblade.

It’s a free browser fantasy arena game combining sword combat, sorcery, and a knight with a rather flexible understanding of honor. Duel other players, fight bots, or learn your craft in the Practice Yard.

Play Swords & Sorcery in your browser · Source and development

I’m still working on the combat, animation, and Castleward itself. The creative direction, character designs, dialogue, and voice performance are mine. AI helps with coding, debugging, Blender tools, and production art.

The game remains free. If you’d like to support my work, you can contribute on Buy Me a Coffee.

# Welcome to Castleward

Prepared for creator approval. Keep as an unpublished introductory itch.io devlog.

Saved private draft: https://captainfredric.itch.io/swords-and-sorcery/devlog/1707848/welcome-to-castleward

I’m CaptainFredric, the creator, designer and voice of the Spellblade in Swords & Sorcery. It’s a free browser multiplayer game about sword combat, sorcery and a knight with a flexible understanding of honor.

Share a room code and duel a friend, find an open match, or fight a bot. Sword contacts, Guard and parries matter. So does knowing when to answer an argument with a Fireball.

Greener Pastures is the optional voiced tutorial. An introduction to another knight becomes a disagreement, then a duel and a chase. The Spellblade even has objections to his own stamina bar: “Why is it going down? I did not order it to.” You can skip the tutorial and enter multiplayer immediately.

I designed the character, combat and dialogue, and perform the Spellblade’s voice. AI has helped with implementation, networking, debugging, testing and Blender tools. Making those parts feel like one game has taken a great deal of playing, questioning and revising. Solo combat now runs locally in the browser; multiplayer keeps the server in charge of combat.

This is a playable pre alpha. I’m continuing to refine the character, tutorial and combat presentation. If you try it, tell me where you first felt confused, whether the controls felt responsive, or what you would like to play again.

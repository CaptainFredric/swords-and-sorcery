I’m CaptainFredric, the creator and designer of Swords & Sorcery and the voice of the Spellblade. It’s a free browser multiplayer game about sword combat, sorcery, and a knight with a rather flexible understanding of honor.

Share a room code and duel a friend, join an open match, or fight a bot. Where your sword hits matters. A well timed block or parry can turn a fight. So can knowing when to answer an argument with a Fireball.

Greener Pastures is the optional, fully voiced tutorial. An introduction to another knight becomes a disagreement, then a duel, and eventually a chase. The Spellblade even has objections to his own stamina bar: “Why is it going down? I did not order it to.” You can skip the tutorial and enter multiplayer immediately.

I designed the character, combat, and dialogue, and perform the Spellblade’s voice. AI has helped with coding, networking, debugging, testing, and Blender tools. Getting all of those parts to feel like one game has taken a lot of playtesting and revision. Solo combat runs in your browser. Multiplayer combat is handled by the server.

The game is playable now and still in active development. I’m continuing to refine the character, tutorial, and combat presentation. If you try it, tell me what confused you, whether the controls felt responsive, and what made you want to keep playing.

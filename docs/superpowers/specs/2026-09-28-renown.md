# Renown milestone
Approved scope: complete a match, earn server verified Renown, buy Azure cloth, equip it, retain it after reload, and replicate its appearance to other players.
Preserve combat strength and current art. Crimson remains free. Azure costs 40. Completed scored server matches with at least 30 seconds and combat participation award 20 plus 10 for a win. Practice, offline play, forfeits and early departure award zero. Guest credentials are independent of room sessions. Purchases and rewards are idempotent and persisted atomically. Preview does not equip or spend.
Local storage is a credential and display cache only. Durable production storage requires a persistent RENOWN_DATA_DIR. Current free Render deployment has no persistent disk. Do not provision paid services or claim deployment persistence without configuring it.

User refinement: Combat Kit opens first. Cosmetic progression lives in a separate Heraldry section.

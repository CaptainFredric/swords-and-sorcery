======================================================================
1. CORE FANTASY
======================================================================

SPELLS & CHIVALRY

Activation line already conceptually belongs to:

    "I am the master... of swords & sorcery!!!"

The mechanic needs to justify that claim.

For 9 seconds, the Spellblade becomes exceptionally competent with the SAME
combat kit rather than gaining generic stats.

Ordinarily, Sword, Guard, Dash and spellcasting compete for action ownership.

During Spells & Chivalry:

    SWORD + GUARD + SPELL + DASH

may operate simultaneously.

In addition:

- recovery between those actions becomes extremely fluid
- ordinary parries still defeat a sword contact but do not impose the special
  mandatory parry reel
- the player gains access to THREE prepared spells chosen in the Armory
- those spells can be switched rapidly during combat
- whichever prepared spell becomes current stays current after the ultimate
  ends, for the remainder of that match

This is NOT:

- +30% everything
- damage resistance
- invulnerability
- infinite Guard
- attack-speed steroid
- movement-speed steroid
- global cooldown reduction
- AoE aura
- a stance slider
- a giant spell wheel
- a paused inventory
- Vortex again

The fantasy is:

    "I no longer have to choose one discipline at a time."


======================================================================
2. ULTIMATE IDENTITY RELATIVE TO SUNDER AND VORTEX
======================================================================

Preserve the three ultimate identities.

SUNDER
    brute physical authority

VORTEX

    transformation
    mobility

SPELLS & CHIVALRY

    concurrency
    rapid tactical adaptation
    technical mastery

Do NOT hard-code a rock-paper-scissors triangle.

But preserve the natural counterplay identities that emerge from these roles.

Spells & Chivalry should not become "the safe best ultimate."

======================================================================
4. STARTUP
======================================================================

Startup should visibly take ownership immediately.

On startup:

- stop ordinary sword continuation cleanly
- lower Guard
- cancel an uncommitted ordinary spell gather
- normalize/end incompatible Dash state according to existing ultimate rules
- deal no startup damage

Blend visually rather than snapping.

Physical held inputs remain intent.

Examples:

hold G through startup
-> Guard becomes active immediately at commit

hold LMB through startup
-> sword may begin immediately at commit


IMPORTANT NEW RULE:

The prepared-spell HUD/repertoire may become visible during startup.

The player may PRESELECT a prepared spell during startup.

They may NOT cast it before commit.

This gives the vulnerable startup a short tactical planning beat without
turning it into a menu.


======================================================================
5. CORE CONCURRENCY
======================================================================

During active Chivalry:

Beginning one action from:

    Sword
    Guard
    current prepared spell
    Dash

does not cancel another legal action in that set.


ATTACK + GUARD

- sword attacks do not lower Guard
- raising Guard does not stop sword chain
- Guard remains mechanically active during attacks
- normal sword geometry/timing/damage remain
- attacking does not refresh perfect-parry timing


ATTACK + SPELL

- spell gather does not stop sword
- sword does not cancel gather
- release uses current authoritative aim/origin


GUARD + SPELL

- spell may gather and release while Guard remains active
- casting does not lower Guard


DASH + ATTACK

- sword remains real spatial contact during Dash
- no bonus Dash-strike damage
- no duplicated sweep


DASH + GUARD

- Guard may remain active through Dash


DASH + SPELL

- gather/release may occur during Dash


ALL FOUR

Sword + Guard + spell + Dash simultaneously is legal.

This must be real authoritative coexistence, not fake rapid state swapping.


======================================================================
6. FAST ACTION FLOW
======================================================================

Chivalry is also about technical fluency.

Allowed actions should transition/recover into each other with very little
dead time.

Target presentation blend where useful:

    ~80–120 ms

Examples:

- sword recovery -> spell
- sword recovery -> Dash
- sword recovery -> Guard
- spell release -> sword
- spell release -> Dash
- Dash -> sword
- Dash -> spell
- Dash -> Guard
- Guard -> sword
- Guard -> spell

IMPORTANT:

If two actions can coexist, prefer coexistence.

Do not cancel one merely because another began.

Fast transitions remove dead recovery, not legitimate offensive clocks.


======================================================================
7. NO ANIMATION-CANCEL DPS
======================================================================

SWORD

Once a sword strike is committed/live:

- it is spent
- it does not reset because another action begins
- it does not duplicate because Dash began
- it does not duplicate because Guard began
- it does not duplicate because a spell began
- the next strike cannot arrive earlier than the ordinary chain permits

Do not create cancel-tech extra DPS.


SPELL

Once a spell gather is accepted:

- one gather exists
- it cannot duplicate
- another action does not reset gather timing
- release happens once
- changing prepared spell cannot duplicate an accepted cast


======================================================================
8. GUARD: ADD G AS A SECOND DEFAULT
======================================================================

Keep:

    RMB = Guard

Add:

    G = Guard

as a second DEFAULT binding.

Do not replace RMB.

Guard remains one logical action with multiple physical sources.


REQUIRED HOLD SEMANTICS

Hold RMB
press G
release RMB

=> Guard stays active because G remains held


Hold G
press RMB
release G

=> Guard stays active because RMB remains held


Release both

=> Guard ends


Pressing a second Guard binding while logical Guard is already active must NOT:

- emit a second Guard-start event
- reset guardStartedAt
- manufacture another perfect-parry window

Touch controls remain unchanged.

Vortex uses logical Guard for Blade emphasis, so either RMB or G may steer it.


======================================================================
9. PARRY RULE DURING CHIVALRY
======================================================================

A successful ordinary parry still:

- defeats that sword contact
- benefits defender normally
- applies normal Balance contribution
- produces parry presentation

BUT:

While committed Spells & Chivalry is active:

    suppress the special mandatory parry action-lock/reel.

The strike loses.

The attacker does not become inert.


This is NOT Stagger immunity.

If the parry's Balance contribution creates a true Balance break:

    real Stagger still happens.

Chivalry does not protect against:

- real Balance break
- Guard Break
- Sunder interruption
- knockback
- death


======================================================================
10. REAL COUNTERPLAY
======================================================================

Spells & Chivalry removes ordinary commitment vulnerability.

It does NOT remove resource or physical vulnerability.

Opponent counterplay should remain:

- exhaust/break Guard
- hit with spells that passive Guard does not solve
- build real Balance break
- Gale the player out of advantageous geometry
- Sunder-interrupt current actions
- disengage and survive the 9-second window
- punish the return to ordinary action exclusivity afterward

Do not add hidden DR to compensate for survivability.

The ultimate's defense is:

    "I can defend while doing other things."

Not:

    "I take less damage because ultimate."


======================================================================
11. PREPARED SPELLS — LOADOUT ARCHETYPE
======================================================================

Spells & Chivalry changes what the player's spell loadout means.

A player who chooses this ultimate prepares THREE spells.

Do NOT conceptually model them as:

    Main
    Reserve I
    Reserve II

Instead model:

    PREPARED SPELLS
    three equal spell identities
    one is CURRENT

Example:

    PREPARED SPELLS

    ★ Fireball
      Frostfire
      Gale Garner

The star/current marker moves.

The prepared SET does not.


======================================================================
12. ARMORY CONFIGURATION
======================================================================

When Spells & Chivalry is selected, its Armory card exposes:

    PREPARED SPELLS

Choose exactly THREE distinct legal spells.

The player's ordinary equipped spell determines which prepared spell begins
as CURRENT when the match begins.

The other two are equally prepared, not lesser reserves.


Example:

Prepared:
- Fireball
- Frostfire
- Gale

Starting/current:
- Fireball


If ordinary starting spell is changed to one already in the set:

preserve three unique prepared spells deterministically rather than allowing
duplicates.

Example:

Prepared set:
Fireball / Frostfire / Gale

Starting:
Fireball

Player makes Frostfire starting spell.

Result:

Prepared set remains:
Fireball / Frostfire / Gale

Current/start marker moves to Frostfire.

Do not needlessly reorder identities.

======================================================================
14. CURRENT SPELL PERSISTENCE
======================================================================

Distinguish:

A. SAVED STARTING SPELL
   chosen in Armory for the next match

B. MATCH CURRENT SPELL
   currently active in live combat


During Chivalry:

switching prepared spell changes MATCH CURRENT SPELL.


WHEN CHIVALRY ENDS:

the selected spell stays current.


WHEN PLAYER DIES AND RESPAWNS IN SAME MATCH:

the selected spell stays current.


ON A LATER CHIVALRY ACTIVATION:

the same prepared set reopens around whatever member is currently selected.


WHEN A NEW MATCH STARTS:

return to SAVED STARTING SPELL.


A tactical in-match switch must NOT silently rewrite the persistent Armory
starting loadout.

16. SPELL SELECTION VS AVAILABILITY
======================================================================

Selection and availability are distinct.

The player may make a prepared spell CURRENT even if that spell is cooling down.

Selection means:

    "This is what Q now attempts to use."

Availability means:

    "It is currently legal to cast."


HUD must show unavailable/cooldown state clearly.

SHEATHE IN STEEL

======================================================================
19. ATOMIC SELECT + CAST
======================================================================

This is important for network correctness.

A player gesture meaning:

    "switch to Frostfire and cast Frostfire"

must be one logical server-authoritative intent.

Do NOT depend on:

packet A:
    set current spell Frostfire

packet B:
    cast current spell

because latency/order can cast the old spell.


Provide an authoritative command equivalent to:

    castPreparedSpell(slot/validated-id, aim/input-state)

Server validates:

- alive
- Chivalry phase allows it
- requested spell belongs to prepared set
- requested spell is legal
- relevant cooldown/gate is ready
- gather rules allow another cast

Then atomically:

- change match current spell
- begin that spell cast


Clients send intent, not outcomes.

======================================================================
37. PURPOSE
======================================================================

Challenges are not merely alternate Renown chores.

They serve TWO purposes:

1. permanent mastery goals
2. an implicit advanced tutorial for systems players may otherwise miss

A good Challenge should make someone discover or deliberately use a real
interaction in the combat system.

Ask:

    "Would the player remember doing this?"

Not:

    "How many more repetitions until the bar fills?"


NO MMO QUEST SLOP.

No:
- Deal 10,000 damage
- Cast Fireball 100 times
- Kill 200 enemies
- daily chores
- weekly FOMO
- login streaks
- reroll currency
- generic grind meters


======================================================================
38. CHALLENGE FAMILIES
======================================================================

Organize Challenges into a few meaningful families.

Recommended initial families:

SWORDSMANSHIP
    sword / Guard / parry mastery

SORCERY
    spell interactions / Gale / Steel

CHIVALRY
    ultimates / mixed-discipline mastery

ODDITIES
    hidden or strange interactions

Do not make categories so granular that each contains one item.

These families should help the player notice:

    "There are systems here I have not explored."


======================================================================
39. VISIBILITY
======================================================================

VISIBLE

- title visible
- real condition visible
- progress visible where useful


HINTED

Before completion:
- real title hidden
- exact condition hidden
- authored hint visible
- no numeric leakage that trivially solves it

After completion:
- reveal title
- reveal exact condition
- mark complete
- reveal reward

Hints may vary deliberately in vagueness.

Do not create an automatic hint-vagueness system.


======================================================================
40. REWARDS
======================================================================

Initial Challenges may primarily award Renown.

BUT:

Do not architect reward as:

    reward: number

Use an extensible reward shape, for example:

    reward: { renown: 20 }

This leaves room later for authored cosmetic rewards such as:

- heraldry
- title
- crest variation
- tabard trim
- badge

NO combat power rewards.

Do not implement a giant cosmetic-reward feature now.

Just avoid hardcoding the schema into "challenge equals integer Renown."


======================================================================
41. AUTHORITY
======================================================================

Challenge progress is server authoritative.

Client does NOT report:

    "I completed challenge X."

Recommended split:

shared/src/challenges.mjs
    player-facing metadata / definitions

shared/sim/challenges.mjs
    authoritative match-local observation/evaluation

server profile persistence
    permanent progress/completion/rewards

Combat code decides combat.

Challenge code observes authoritative facts.

Profile code persists settled results.


======================================================================

- Practice

- forfeits
- players who leave before eligible settlement

Challenge reward applies automatically.

NO claim button.

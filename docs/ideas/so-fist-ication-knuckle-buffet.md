# SO-FIST-ICATION — Knuckle Buffet

Status: future candidate equipped Siege Skill; not implemented or commissioned. Authored November 26, 2026. Preserve the creator's name.

## Core concept

- Rapid continuous piston punches, approximately 2.5 punches per second.
- The machine must be stationary and must have remained stationary for a brief interval before activation. Stopping immediately before activation is insufficient.
- While active, the attack continuously consumes the machine's charge (Drive Pressure). It is repeatable, rather than a one-shot committed attack.
- Intended to excel as a siege breaker against machines and stationary defenses.
- Exact stationary dwell time, pressure cost, damage per punch, stagger, Guard pressure, recoil, reach, interruption and cooldown rules are not finalized.

## Design considerations (not commitments)

Preserve the physical character of successive punches; ordinary knockback may naturally push mobile targets outside reach. Consider weaker individual impacts than ordinary committed piston strikes, with cumulative effectiveness against targets that remain within reach. Avoid assuming these suggestions are approved balance rules.

Do not add this candidate to the playable Skill registry or alter the existing three Skills until a dedicated implementation is commissioned.
